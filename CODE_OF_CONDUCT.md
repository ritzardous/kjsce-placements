# Code of Conduct

We want a respectful, useful community where people can contribute technical work, source corrections and firsthand experiences without harassment.

## Expected participation

Be considerate and direct. Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people. Give actionable feedback, acknowledge uncertainty, respect different backgrounds and experience levels, and accept good-faith corrections. Keep project spaces focused on relevant collaboration.

Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish personal information without permission, impersonate others, post sexualized content, or repeatedly disrupt discussion. Do not publish student contact details, credentials or private moderation material. Anonymous experience attribution is a supported display choice and must not invite attempts to identify or target its author.

## Scope and moderation

These expectations apply in repository issues, pull requests, discussions and project-managed community content, including public spaces where a person represents the project. Maintainers may edit/remove content, restrict participation or close discussions to protect participants. They should explain decisions when appropriate while preserving reporter privacy, consider context and severity, and avoid participating in enforcement decisions where they have a conflict.

For minor problems, a clarification or request to change behavior may be sufficient. Serious or repeated misconduct may result in temporary or permanent participation restrictions. Reports should be handled discreetly; confidentiality cannot be guaranteed beyond the people and systems needed to investigate.

## Reporting and review

A dedicated private conduct contact has not yet been designated. Do not post sensitive reports in public issues. If you already have an established private channel with a maintainer, use it and request a safe reporting route. Maintainers must publish a contact and a process for reviewing contested decisions; no response deadline or independent appeal body is currently promised.

Security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). This project-specific policy does not claim adoption of an external conduct framework or an enforcement team that has not been established.
