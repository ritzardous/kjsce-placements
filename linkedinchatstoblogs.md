The selection process started with an online assessment (OA) consisting of 8 questions - 2 DSA questions, 1 SQL question, and the remaining 5 were MCQs.

OA Questions

1. Maximum Sensor Listening Score
   You are given T time slots and N sensors.

For every time slot, you may listen to at most M sensors simultaneously.

Each sensor has a global cooldown period D. If a sensor is listened to during time slot t, it cannot be listened to again until time slot t + D + 1 or later.

You are also given a matrix score of size T × N, where score[i][j] represents the score obtained by listening to sensor j during time slot i.

It is guaranteed that for every sensor, the scores are monotonically non-increasing over time.

Your task is to maximize the total score obtained across all time slots.

Constraints:

- At each time slot, you may listen to at most M sensors.
- A sensor cannot be selected again until its cooldown period expires.
- Each selected sensor contributes its score for that time slot.

Return the maximum possible score.

Function Signature:
long long maximumScore(int T, int N, int M, int D, vector<vector<int>>& score);

Constraints:
1 ≤ T × N ≤ 10^5
1 ≤ M ≤ N
0 ≤ D < T
0 ≤ score[i][j] ≤ 10^9

Example:

T = 4
N = 3
M = 2
D = 1

score =
[
[5,4,3],
[4,4,3],
[3,2,2],
[2,1,1]
]

Optimal Selection

Time Slot 1 -> Sensors {1,2} : 5 + 4 = 9
Time Slot 2 -> Sensor {3} : 3
Time Slot 3 -> Sensors {1,2} : 3 + 2 = 5
Time Slot 4 -> Sensor {3} : 1

Total Score = 9 + 3 + 5 + 1 = 18

Output:
18

2. Course Completion Statistics (SQL)
   You are given the following tables.

Employee

- employee_id (Primary Key)
- employee_name

TrainingCourse

- course_id (Primary Key)
- course_name
- course_duration

TrainingResult

- employee_id
- course_id
- score

Write an SQL query to generate a report containing:

- course_id
- course_name
- total_employees
- completion_rate

Where:

- total_employees is the total number of employees who attempted the course.
- completion_rate = (Number of employees with score > 50 / Total employees who attempted the course) × 100.

Round completion_rate to 2 decimal places.

Example

Employee
employee_id | employee_name
1 | Alice
2 | Bob
3 | Charlie

TrainingCourse
course_id | course_name | course_duration

TrainingResult

course_id | course_name | total_employees | completion_rate
