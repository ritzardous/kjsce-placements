# About-page legal notice

[Features](features.md) · [Security architecture](security.md) · [Maintainer decisions](maintainer-decisions.md)

The public About page preserves the story and developer introduction, followed by numbered sections for source limitations, personal-data processing and conditions of use. The maintained text is [ProjectLegalNotice.tsx](../src/app/ProjectLegalNotice.tsx). Existing section URLs remain valid. Decorative uppercase subtitles have been removed.

The notice describes implemented controls and their limits. It does not implement consent management, college-domain restrictions, age verification, a retention schedule, a statutory-officer appointment or a complaint-handling backend. It does not certify compliance, establish permission to republish records or guarantee enforceability of a disclaimer. Signing in is not consent on behalf of people in source documents.

The notice preserves mandatory remedies, access to competent courts/regulators and liabilities that cannot lawfully be excluded. It introduces no arbitrary liability cap, indemnity, exclusive jurisdiction, mandatory arbitration or retrospective consent. Submission is described as a request for review/publication; contributions are not silently relicensed under MIT.

## Outstanding legal and operational review

This is a draft notice grounded in the implementation, not a legal opinion. Qualified Indian counsel and maintainers should confirm:

- Responsible operator identity, contact/address particulars, legal status, statutory duties and any required named grievance contact or officer.
- Lawful processing/publication authority for each source category, including repository copies and Git history. Website sign-in does not protect those copies.
- Record-specific consent/permissions and withdrawal where required. A notice cannot supply missing authority or authorise a materially different future purpose.
- Concrete retention, backup, correction/removal, grievance, incident/breach and statutory-request procedures and applicable deadlines.
- Provider configuration, transfers, processing arrangements, logging, analytics notice/consent and age-related safeguards.
- Enforceability of conditions and liability limitations, the acceptance mechanism, and mandatory consumer/data-protection rights where applicable.
- Rights in source documents, employer marks and contributor material. A code licence is not a licence to personal records or third-party assets.

Re-review when laws commence or change, processing/provider settings change, or commercial arrangements are introduced. No underlying permissions, database access, retention implementation or authentication rules were changed by this copy update.
